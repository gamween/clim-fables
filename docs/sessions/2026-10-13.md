## Security review of the v2 contracts (2026-10-13, evening)

A review of `RiskDeskV2`, `ClimFeePolicy`, `ClimPoker` and `PokeFeeHook` for Fables' CTO. The maintainer decided every fix below; each one went in test first.

- **C1 (critical) + I1, ClimPoker access:** a permissionless `poke()` let anyone overwrite the keeper's override with clim's lower fee, since Fables' hook has one override slot per pool and their keeper keeps poking it directly; the poker was also `Ownable2Step` with clim as owner. Now `ClimPoker` is `AccessManaged` by Fables' own AccessManager: `poke()`, `postEvent()` and the new `postEventAndPoke()` are `restricted`, `eventKeeper` and `setEventKeeper` are gone, and the constructor's last argument is the authority. Level 2 is one batch on Fables' side: `grantRole(FEE_POKER, poker, 0)`, `setTargetFunctionRole(poker, [postEvent, postEventAndPoke], FEE_POKER)`, `labelRole(7, "CLIM_RUNNER")` and `setTargetFunctionRole(poker, [poke], CLIM_RUNNER)`, CLIM_RUNNER granted to clim's runner and to their keeper (7 is the id used in tests). clim holds no admin power; revoking FEE_POKER from the poker stops everything. `postEvent` rejects an expiry beyond 72 h (`InvalidExpiry`).
- **M1, M2, TTL:** the TTL sent to the hook ends at `tObs + ttl` (anchored to the observation, not to the poke), never exceeds `ttl` (a future `tObs` within the 30 s skew does not extend it), ends with a live event that raised either side, and is at least 1 s. `ClimFeePolicy.lastObservation()` exposes the desk's `tObs` and `seq`.
- **M3, envelope after an outage:** a report more than `RESET_GAP` = 180 s after the last one is taken as is (absolute bounds only), not ramped from a stale sigma.
- **M6:** `PokeFeeHook.setPoolConfig` reverts with `InvalidConfig` when the key names another hook.
- **M7, events:** `Poked(seq, tObs, fee0For1, fee1For0, mode, eventApplied, expiry, caller)`, `EventPosted(by, ...)`, and `RiskReportedV2` carries `flags`. `eventApplied` now means the event raised at least one side.
- **M8:** `postEvent` NatSpec fixed; the blind check compares to a file-level `POLICY_MODE_BLIND` constant (solc 0.8.26 does not expose `ClimFeePolicy.MODE_BLIND` to another contract, so the constants moved to file level and the contract re-exports them as `MODE_*`); `ClimFeeMath` no longer points at ClimHook alone.
- **I3, leaving simulation mode:** `disableSim(prodForwarder)` requires a non-zero forwarder and a pinned workflow (id or author), sets the forwarder and switches `simMode` off in the same call. This needed one word in the vendored `ReceiverTemplate.sol`: `setForwarderAddress` is `public` instead of `external` (no ABI change, commented in place).
- **I4:** `RiskDeskV2` NatSpec no longer claims there is no setter at all: the owner cannot set sigma or the gap, but picks the forwarder and workflow, so ownership goes to a timelock or is renounced once configured.
- **I5, direction:** `ClimFeePolicy.Params.ethIsToken0` (immutable). A positive gap makes ETH buyers toxic: one-for-zero when ETH is currency0 (ETH/USDG, `true`), zero-for-one otherwise. Tested both ways.
- **I6:** "clean-room" replaced everywhere by "independent implementation written from Fables' published ABI and documented behavior; no Fables code is copied".

### Security notes for docs/security.md (plan 04)

- **Hook band versus policy band:** if Fables lowers the hook's cap below the policy's cap, or raises its floor above the policy's floor, pokes outside the new band revert in the hook; the pool keeps its last override until it expires, then its flat fee. Deployment invariant: the policy band sits inside the hook band (fork-tested with a 3,000-pip cap).
- **Desk owner trust:** the owner cannot set sigma or the gap, but chooses the forwarder and the workflow (id, author, name), which decides what is reported. Once configured, ownership goes to a timelock or is renounced. `ReceiverTemplate` is one-step `Ownable` (no two-step transfer), so a transfer to a wrong address is final.
- **Blind versus degraded:** blind (no report for more than `tauKillSec`) means no poke at all, and the pool returns to its flat 7 bp when the last override expires; degraded (venues disagree beyond 25 bp) still pokes, at least the safe fee (30 bp).
- **The keeper through ClimPoker:** an event can only raise a side (`max(policy fee, event fee)`, clamped to the policy cap), never lower clim's fee. If Fables' keeper keeps poking the hook directly while CLIM_RUNNER is granted, the last writer wins in the hook's single slot; the intended setup routes their event overrides through `postEvent` / `postEventAndPoke`.
- **Symmetric hooks:** a single-fee hook gets the higher of the two sides, so a directional premium would raise both directions; keep `dirSlopeE4 = 0` for ETH/USDG until it moves to a per-direction hook.
- **Clock skew:** a report may carry a `tObs` up to 30 s in the future, which extends its freshness window (`tObs + tauKillSec`) by as much; the TTL sent to the hook is capped at `ttl`, so the override itself is not extended.
- **Simulation mode:** while `simMode` is on, the trust anchor is the operator EOA (`tx.origin` guard), since the mock forwarder checks no signature. `disableSim(prodForwarder)` ends it in one call, only once the workflow is pinned.
